# Episode 2 — "Left Line" — Voiceover script

Narrator: Ksyusha. One voice (ElevenLabs "Jessica", eleven_v3) reads everything, including Dad's and Tyoma's lines.
Cues in [square brackets] are not spoken. The exact text sent to ElevenLabs is `voice/full_v3.txt`.

```
[quietly] At six the gates closed... [pause] I was on the wrong side of them.
[low, indifferent] "Quarantine, just for a day," [tired] the guard said, and walked the left line into the tunnel under the stands.
[nervously] But the door locked behind us... [pause] from the outside.
[whispers] The man next to me was shivering — fever comes first.
[sad] That voice in the stands was Dad, who was called here three days ago and never came home.
[determined] They checked for bites, not for iron — the crowbar was still in my pocket.
[whispers] Above the benches was a vent, but the crowbar is loud, and they listen.
[tense] So I waited... [pause] and when the convoy started its engines, I tore the grate off under the roar.
[scared whisper] Behind me, the shivering man lifted his head... [pause] and stopped shivering.
[out of breath] The shaft led to the top of the stands, and below, the convoy drove out without us.
[relieved] In the last row sat Dad, holding Tyoma's hand.
[whispers, childlike] "I ran, just like he said," Tyoma whispered.
[low, tired] "The clear ones go to the base," Dad said, "and nobody comes back."
[pause] Then Tyoma asked his question: [innocently] "Dad, why is your hand bandaged?"
[long pause] [quietly] Dad pulled his sleeve down.
[whispers] And below us, in the tunnel... [pause] someone started banging on the door.
```
