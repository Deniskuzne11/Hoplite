# Episode 1 — "06:00" — Voiceover script

Narrator: Ksyusha, 16. One voice reads everything, including the quoted lines.
Cues in [square brackets] are not spoken.

```
[quietly, slowly] We were given until six in the morning. [pause]
[flat, tired] The radio repeated it three times: last convoy, the stadium, one wristband — one seat.
[a little warmer] I had two wristbands, a crowbar, and Tyoma, who never stops asking questions.
[whispering] The bridge was the only way to the stadium — [pause] and they were standing on it.
[whispering, childlike] "Why are they just standing there?" [pause] [whispering, tense] "They're listening, Tyoma."
[calmer, confident] So we went through the courtyards. I've known every one of them since I was five.
[anxiously] But the last passage was boarded up. [pause] The board came off with a crash, [faster] and the whole street heard it.
[through the teeth] A nail tore my palm. [pause] [whispering, scared] Behind us, on the bridge, they all turned at once.
[out of breath, fast] We ran — straight into two men with rifles. [low, rough] "Wristband. The kid's. He'll manage without one."
[pause] [slowly] Then Tyoma asked his question: [childlike, innocent] "Mister, why are you hiding your hand?"
[whispering] Under the sleeve was a bite. [fast] His partner stepped back and fired. [anxiously] The gunshot brought the whole bridge after them.
[quietly, short phrases] Five fifty-two. Stadium. Floodlights. [pause] [cold] They weren't evacuating anyone. They were sorting people.
[pause] [flat, numb] The guard shone his flashlight on my palm: [low, indifferent] "To the left."
[whispering, faster] And from the dark stands, a voice I knew shouted to my brother: [long pause] [shout, held back] "Run."
```
