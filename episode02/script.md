# Episode 2 — "Left Line" — Step 1: Script (draft)

Continues straight from Episode 1: 05:59, Ksyusha is in the LEFT ("bitten") line with a nail wound, not a bite.
Tyoma is alone in the RIGHT ("clear") line. A voice she knows shouted "Run." from the dark stands.

## Logic decisions
- **Who shouted:** their father. He is an ambulance paramedic who was called to the stadium three days ago
  and never came home. That is why Ksyusha was taking Tyoma alone in Episode 1.
- **Why "Run":** the "clear" are not evacuated either. They go to "the base", and nobody comes back. (Hook for episode 3.)
- **The crowbar stays:** the guards checked for bites, not for iron. It is still in her back pocket.
- **The sound rule pays off:** she can't pry the vent grate open quietly, so she waits for the 06:00 convoy
  engines to cover the noise.
- **Fever = turning:** the shivering man in the tunnel shows the viewer the sign. At the end, Dad wipes sweat
  off his forehead and has a bandaged hand.
- **Tyoma's question is the weapon again:** "Dad, why is your hand bandaged?" echoes "Mister, why are you hiding your hand?".
- **Timeline:** 05:59 → 06:00 (gates close, convoy leaves) → 06:10. Dawn, light rain.
