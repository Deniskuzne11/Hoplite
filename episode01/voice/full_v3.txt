[quietly] We were given until six in the morning... [pause]
[tired] The radio repeated it three times: last convoy, the stadium, one wristband — one seat.
I had two wristbands, a crowbar, and Tyoma, who never stops asking questions.
[whispers] The bridge was the only way to the stadium... [pause] and they were standing on it.
[whispers] "Why are they just standing there?" [pause] "They're listening, Tyoma."
[calm] So we went through the courtyards. I've known every one of them since I was five.
[nervously] But the last passage was boarded up. [pause] The board came off with a crash, and the whole street heard it.
[in pain] A nail tore my palm. [pause] [scared whisper] Behind us, on the bridge, they all turned at once.
[out of breath] We ran — straight into two men with rifles. [low, rough voice] "Wristband. The kid's. He'll manage without one."
[pause] Then Tyoma asked his question: [innocently] "Mister, why are you hiding your hand?"
[whispers] Under the sleeve was a bite. [tense] His partner stepped back and fired. The gunshot brought the whole bridge after them.
[quietly] Five fifty-two. Stadium. Floodlights. [pause] [cold] They weren't evacuating anyone. They were sorting people.
[pause] [numb] The guard shone his flashlight on my palm: [indifferent] "To the left."
[whispers] And from the dark stands, a voice I knew shouted to my brother... [long pause] [shouts] "Run."
